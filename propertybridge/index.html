Financing engine

Possible deal paths

The engine should identify which path is plausible, then tell us what must be verified before we rely on it.

Assumption

Existing FHA/VA/USDA financing, lender/servicer approval and buyer qualification.

Seller Finance

Owner carries some or all of the purchase price under negotiated documented terms.

Wholesale / Assignment

Contract-control strategy only where the contract and applicable state law permit it.

Rental / DSCR

Property cash flow supports investor financing after lender underwriting.

Partner Capital

Equity or acquisition partner fills a funding gap under clear written economics.
