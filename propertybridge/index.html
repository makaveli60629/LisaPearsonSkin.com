CAPITAL & DUE DILIGENCE

Financing Paths

Use the financing board to identify plausible routes, then verify lender, title, legal, property and cash-flow facts before relying on the strategy.

Assumption

Existing FHA/VA/USDA financing, lender/servicer approval and buyer qualification.

Seller Finance

Owner carries some or all of the price under negotiated documented terms.

Wholesale / Assignment

Contract-control strategy only where the contract and applicable law permit it.

Rental / DSCR

Property cash flow supports investor financing after lender underwriting.

Partner Capital

Equity or acquisition partner fills a gap under clear written economics.

Land + Modular

Verify zoning, utility access, septic/well, road access, permits, site work and total installed cost.
