Project brief

SVR Poker

A multi-platform poker experience focused on browser-based play, WebXR and Meta Quest, with Android and mobile fallbacks and a larger immersive-game roadmap.

Open SVR PokerBack to Projects

Platform direction

  • Meta Quest hands-first VR.
  • Android WebXR / magic-window support.
  • iPhone touch/spatial fallback.
  • Direct table gameplay and multiplayer infrastructure.

Current priorities

  • Scene lighting and table readability.
  • Hand/watch orientation and interaction polish.
  • Performance and asset-loading reliability.
  • Progressive deployment without regressing stable baselines.
