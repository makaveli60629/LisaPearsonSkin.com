Project brief • Working MVP

A real-estate acquisition command center.

PROPERTYBridge is designed to connect deal discovery, property history, financing strategy, seller context, buyer demand and follow-up into one operating system. The goal is not just to collect listings; it is to reduce uncertainty and move qualified opportunities through a repeatable process.

SOURCE → VERIFY → PROPERTY DNA → DEAL SCORE → FINANCE → BUYER / PARTNER MATCH → CLOSE

Current working functions

  • Manual real-property lead intake.
  • Opportunity board with strategy filters.
  • Property DNA workflow and confidence labeling.
  • Prototype deal scoring.
  • Seller CRM and buyer buy boxes.
  • Property Watch and manual-review outreach drafts.

Production direction

  • Licensed listing/property-data feed.
  • Public-record and deed/tax integrations.
  • Shared authentication and database.
  • Automatic price/status/history monitoring.
  • Buyer-match alerts and deal rooms.
  • Documented compliance and state-specific deal rules.
