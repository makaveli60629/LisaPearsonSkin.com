PROPERTYBridge Network

A real-estate opportunity platform designed to connect motivated sellers, home buyers, investors and creative-financing opportunities around one property-intelligence workflow.

SCAN → PROPERTY DNA → SELLER CRM → FINANCING PATH → BUYER MATCH → DEAL ROOM

Core system

  • Assumable and seller-finance opportunity filters.
  • Property history and confidence labeling.
  • Seller CRM and investor buy boxes.
  • Financing-path analysis and deal matching.

Development direction

  • Shared database and account login.
  • Licensed property-data integrations.
  • Automated lead monitoring.
  • Deal rooms, outreach queues and matching alerts.
