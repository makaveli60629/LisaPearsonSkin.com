Affiliate storefront engine

Sell through approved partner links without stocking the product yourself.

The storefront can group approved affiliate products into branded collections. The merchant handles checkout, inventory, shipping and returns; the site earns commission according to the partner program's terms.

Partner connection rule: product cards should use the affiliate network's approved tracking links, images and disclosure language after the business is accepted into that program. The storefront structure is ready for those IDs; no unapproved tracking codes are being invented.
