FUTURE PROGRAM
Organization design

One mission. Clear legal and financial separation.

The project is intentionally designed to avoid mixing charitable funds, ministry funds and family/business assets.

Core rule

Separate books. Separate accounts. Separate purposes.

Each entity should have its own governing documents, EIN, banking, accounting, contracts and records. Transactions between related entities must be documented and handled at arm’s length when required.

Thy Kingdom Come Community ChurchMinistry, worship, religious programs, congregation and church operations.
Kingdom Community Restoration FoundationPlanned charitable service nonprofit for grants, community programs, partnerships and program reporting.
Family / for-profit sidePrivate assets, operating businesses and family trust activity remain outside church and charitable accounts.
Future campus operationsAny revenue-producing RV, laundry, store or commercial activity should be structured and accounted for based on legal, tax and funding requirements instead of being casually mixed into donations.

Startup roadmap

Build the institution before scaling the campus.

The sequencing is designed to make the project grant-ready, bankable and accountable instead of rushing into property before the organization is prepared.

PHASE 1

Governance & Formation

Finalize mission, board roles, bylaws, conflict-of-interest policy, recordkeeping, state formation strategy, EINs, banking and accounting procedures.

PHASE 2

Public Launch & Fundraising Readiness

Complete official domain/communications, donation controls, volunteer policies, project budget, grant calendar, community-needs narrative and sponsor materials.

PHASE 3

Pilot Programs

Start low-cost outreach and partnership programs that can be documented with real participation, expenses, outcomes and community demand.

PHASE 4

Property & Campus Due Diligence

Evaluate land only after zoning, RV rules, utilities, septic/water, access, insurance, flood/environmental conditions, construction costs and financing sources are verified.

PHASE 5

Expansion

Scale programs, housing and facilities state-by-state only after the pilot demonstrates responsible operations and sustainable funding.

Funding architecture

Stack funding by purpose.

No single grant is expected to finance the entire vision. The plan separates donations, charitable grants, community-facility financing, agriculture support and commercial financing according to the activity being funded.
