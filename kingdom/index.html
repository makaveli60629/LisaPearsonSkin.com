Community project • Planning stage

Faith. People. Restoration. Community. Housing. Hope.

Thy Kingdom Come is being organized as a faith-centered community initiative focused on service, restoration, housing/community support and future programs that help people rebuild. The public site is intentionally limited until governance, program structure and organizational status are formally established.

PLANNING • NOT YET REPRESENTING ACTIVE TAX-EXEMPT PROGRAMS

Ministry

Faith, worship, volunteer service and community connection through Thy Kingdom Come Community Church.

Restoration

Future supportive programs for people restarting after homelessness, rehabilitation, illness or major life disruption.

Community Development

Long-term plans for housing, employment pathways, food/garden programs and service-oriented community facilities.
